# Risk of bias: JBI checklist for prevalence studies, decision rules for people who use kratom (v1.0)

Source: Munn et al. (2014, https://doi.org/10.15171/ijhpm.2014.71; 2015, https://doi.org/10.1097/XEB.0000000000000054). Each item: **yes / no / unclear / NA**. The AI assistant drafts each rating with a supporting quote and its location; the draft is stored unchanged. R1 verifies every item and decides (protocol §14). No total score. Items 2, 6 and 9 feed the sensitivity analyses. Adapted from `papers/sports-betting-prevalence/forms/rob_jbi.md`.

The target population is the population of people who use kratom the study claims to describe (kratom users of a district, of a country, of a treatment service). For kratom users within population surveys, items 1, 2 and 9 are rated on the parent survey.

| # | JBI question | Yes | No | Unclear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Was the sample frame appropriate to address the target population? | The frame covers the target population: household or village register; all patients of the named service; all enrolled students | Only part of it: one advocacy group's members for "US kratom consumers", one village for a province | Frame not described |
| 2 | Were study participants sampled in an appropriate way? | Probability sampling, census of the frame, or consecutive patients of a service | Convenience, snowball, respondent-driven without weights, online opt-in | Method not described |
| 3 | Was the sample size adequate? | n of people who use kratom ≥ 246 (±5 percentage points at an expected 20%) | Below 246 | — |
| 4 | Were the subjects and the setting described in detail? | Age, sex, setting, country and dates of data collection reported | Two or more missing | One missing |
| 5 | Was the data analysis conducted with sufficient coverage of the identified sample? | Missing dependence data ≤ 10% of kratom users, or handled | > 10% missing, not handled | Not reported |
| 6 | Were valid methods used for the identification of the condition? | Diagnostic interview, or DSM-5 / DSM-IV / ICD criteria assessed item by item, or a dependence scale with a cut-off validated in people who use kratom | Ad hoc items, study-specific or borrowed cut-offs without validation in kratom users, ASSIST used as a diagnosis | Version or threshold unclear |
| 7 | Was the condition measured in a standard, reliable way for all participants? | Same instrument, time frame, mode and threshold for everyone | Different instruments or modes across participants without a check | Not reported |
| 8 | Was there appropriate statistical analysis? | Numerator and denominator of kratom users clear; CI given or calculable | Percentages that cannot be traced to a numerator and a denominator of kratom users | — |
| 9 | Was the response rate adequate, and if not, was the low response rate managed appropriately? | Response ≥ 60%, or responders compared with non-responders, or weights for non-response | Response < 60% without handling; open online surveys with no denominator of invited people | Not reported |

Additional coding, not part of the JBI rating: `recruitment_channel` (advocacy or vendor channels risk self-selection of satisfied consumers) and `industry_funding`.
